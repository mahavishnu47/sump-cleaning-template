Frequently asked questions

How much does sump cleaning cost in Chennai?

Underground sump cleaning starts at Rs.1,999 for tanks up to 5,000 litres. Overhead tanks start at Rs.1,499. Call 98410 45678 for an exact quote.

How long does a sump cleaning take?

An overhead tank takes 60-90 minutes. An underground sump of 5,000 litres takes 2-3 hours. We confirm the duration before starting.

Do I need to empty the tank before you arrive?

No. Our crew drains the tank mechanically as step one. You only need to give access to the tank, a power point and a water connection.

Are your cleaning chemicals safe for drinking water?

Yes. We use food-grade disinfectants compliant with IS 10500 for potable water, rinsed and residue-checked before refill.
