Service questions, answered

Overhead or sump - which needs cleaning more often?

Underground sumps collect silt faster and need cleaning every 6 months in Chennai conditions. Overhead tanks need it every 6-12 months, or sooner if you see algae, smell or discolouration.

Do you clean Sintex plastic tanks?

Yes - Sintex, concrete, stainless steel and loft tanks. Soft nylon brushes only, never metal, so the lining is never damaged.

Is there any human entry into the tank?

No. The full process is mechanised - pumps, vacuums and jets. Entry, if ever needed for large commercial sumps, is only by trained staff with harness, gas meter and blower.

What equipment do you bring?

150-bar high-pressure jet, 50-litre industrial wet vacuum, UVC lamp, food-grade disinfectants, scrub brushes, and confined-space safety kit. You provide only a power point and water access.

Do you give a bill and service report?

Yes - GST bill plus before/after photos and a digital service checklist on WhatsApp after every job.

Do you take annual contracts for apartments?

Yes. Quarterly AMC plans for associations, schools, hospitals and offices with scheduled slots and per-visit reports. Call 98410 45678 for a custom quote.
